Pixel Belt: Flow Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Babar Imran (Bravo's Games). Contact: Papajohn3432@gmail.com

1. What the game stores on your device

Pixel Belt saves your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, daily streak, missions, collection), your settings (music, sound, haptics, reduced motion) and your purchase entitlements (for example Remove Ads) in the app's private storage on your phone. This data is not uploaded by the game. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. No accounts

The game has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or location.

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, your IP address, device and app information and ad interactions. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Where required, a consent form (Google UMP) is shown before ads are requested. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Rewarded ads are always started by you. Players who buy Remove Ads no longer see interstitial ads.

4. Purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

5. Analytics

The game does not send analytics to us.

6. Children

Pixel Belt is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data deletion

All game data is stored only on your device. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. Data held by Google (advertising) is controlled through your Google account's ad settings.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
